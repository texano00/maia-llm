Maia LLM

Run open-weight language models on a GPU you rent only for as long as you use it. Free and fully open — no account with us, no data selling. You bring your own provider keys and pay providers directly for the compute you use.

Open Maia LLM

Runs in your browser · free · source on GitHub

  • No password to remember. Type your email, receive a one-time code, and your models and chats are waiting. The address is only the key to your data — nothing is sent to you except the access code, and nothing is shared with analytics.
  • Your keys stay yours. Provider keys are stored encrypted and used only to talk to the provider you chose.
  • Content is never sold. Prompts and answers are not analysed, profiled or shared. Optional usage measurements are content-free and off by default.
